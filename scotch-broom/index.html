Scotch Broom

Poetry Broadsides

Scotch Broom is a monthly public art project from Bainbridge Island Press. Each month we print a single poem as a letterpress-style broadside and place copies in Free Little Libraries across Bainbridge Island.

The series takes its name from the island’s familiar invasive shrub—bright, stubborn, and hard to miss— a reminder that poetry can take root in unexpected places. Pick one up, leave one for a neighbor, or download any past issue below.
