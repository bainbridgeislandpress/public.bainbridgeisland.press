Submissions

Scotch Broom & POETICS

We welcome submissions and collaboration on both of our public art projects: Scotch Broom and POETICS Zine. Whether you're a poet, printer, or artist, if you have poems, art, ideas, or want to collaborate on design or printing please reach out.

To submit, email your work or idea to hello@bainbridgeisland.press. Please include:

  • Which project you are interest in (Scotch Broom, POETICS, or either)
  • Your poem(s) or idea in the body of the email or as a PDF attachment
  • Your name as you would like it to appear in print
  • A brief bio (optional)

We read all submissions carefully. If your work is selected, we will be in touch about publication and placement across Bainbridge Island.

Questions? Write us at the same address— hello@bainbridgeisland.press.
